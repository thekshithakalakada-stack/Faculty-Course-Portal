Academic Terminology

Credit Hour
A standard unit of measure representing 50-60 minutes of lecture or 2 hours of laboratory work per week.
Core Course
A compulsory course that every student enrolled in the programme must successfully complete.
Elective Course
A specialized course chosen by students according to their academic interest and career path.
